How to Play Yamb

Yamb is a dice game similar to Yahtzee. Players roll 5 dice up to 3 times per turn, trying to fill in their scoresheet with the best possible combinations.

Equipment

  • 5 six-sided dice
  • A scoresheet for each player

Basic Gameplay

  1. Roll all 5 dice - This is your first roll
  2. Keep or re-roll - Set aside any dice you want to keep and re-roll the rest
  3. Roll again (optional) - You can roll up to 3 times total per turn
  4. Fill in your scoresheet - Choose a cell to write your score based on the column rules.

Note: You may elect to fill in a cell for which you do not meet the criteria, for example by filling in the square for a straight when you did not roll a straight. In this case, fill in 0.

The Scoresheet

The scoresheet has 12 rows (scoring categories) and 4 columns (fill order rules).

Columns (Fill Order)

Column Rule
↓ Down Fill from top to bottom. You must fill the next empty row in order.
↑ Up Fill from bottom to top. You must fill the next empty row in order.
L (Libre) Free column. Fill any empty row in any order.
S (Single) You can only fill a cell in this column after your first roll (no re-rolls allowed).

Rows (Scoring Categories)

Number Rows (1-6)

For each number row, write down how many dice show that number in each column. The target is 12 total across all 4 columns (i.e., 3 per column on average). You score points for each die above or below this target.

Row Points per die over/under 12
1s ±10 points
2s ±20 points
3s ±30 points
4s ±40 points
5s ±50 points
6s ±60 points

Examples for 1s row:

  • You rolled 3+3+3+3 = 12 ones → exactly 12, so 0 points
  • You rolled 4+4+4+4 = 16 ones → 4 over, so 4 × 10 = +40 points
  • You rolled 2+2+3+2 = 9 ones → 3 under, so 3 × 10 = -30 points

Example for 3s row:

  • You rolled 4+3+2+3 = 12 threes → exactly 12, so 0 points
  • You rolled 4+4+3+4 = 15 threes → 3 over, so 3 × 30 = +90 points

High/Low Rows

Row Description Scoring
H (High) Aim for the highest total Sum of all 5 dice (max 30)
L (Low) Aim for the highest total (must be lower than H) Sum of all 5 dice (max 29)

Bonus: If H + L ≥ 50 in a column, you get +30 bonus points for that column. Note that in the scroresheet, any bonus points will be added in the total column at the end.

Special case: If you fill in one of H or L in a column but then can't roll a valid value for the other (i.e., you can't roll higher than L or lower than H), you must change your first entry to 0 and also fill the other with 0.

Combination Rows

Row Required Scoring
FH (Full House) Three of a kind + a pair 40 points
C (Carré) Four of a kind 60 points
S (Straight) Five consecutive numbers (1-2-3-4-5 or 2-3-4-5-6) 80 points
P (Poker) Five of a kind (all 5 dice the same) 100 points

If you don't achieve the required combination, you must write 0 for that cell.

Winning

The game ends when all cells are filled. Add up all your scores from each column. The player with the highest total wins!
